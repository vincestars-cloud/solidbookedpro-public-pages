The Process

From campaign to confirmed calendar.

01

Fit and market review

We start by confirming your licensing, target states, appointment capacity, minimum household fit, and annuity focus. The goal is to avoid launching campaigns where the economics do not make sense.

02

Offer and positioning setup

We frame your appointment around the retirement concerns prospects already feel: protected income, rollover decisions, downside risk, tax-aware planning, and confidence in retirement.

03

Campaigns generate interested prospects

Prospects enter through educational campaigns and are routed into a qualification path before they can reach your calendar.

04

Pre-call nurture and qualification

They receive context, reminders, and follow-up designed to increase show rate and filter out people who are not ready for a real discussion.

05

Appointments land on your calendar

You see the scheduled appointment details, notes, and prospect context. You only pay for appointments that actually show up.

FAQs

Common questions before getting started.

What counts as a show?

A show is a prospect who attends the scheduled appointment window and is present for the consultation. If they no-show, you are not billed for that appointment.

Do you replace my sales process?

No. SolidBooked Pro focuses on acquisition, qualification, booking, reminders, and pre-call education. Your advisory process, compliance language, and close remain yours.

Can this work in my state or niche?

That depends on licensing, market size, competition, and your appointment capacity. The opt-in form is designed to screen those factors before a launch discussion.

How fast can appointments start?

Most campaigns need setup, approval, and optimization time. A realistic launch conversation covers assets, calendar availability, tracking, and the first testing window.
